<section id="research">
  <h2>Research</h2>
  <p>My research focuses on <b>collaborative machine learning</b>, where multiple nodes jointly train models without sharing raw data. My early work studied Byzantine-robust aggregation in federated learning, where a central server coordinates collaboration. I then explored improving model mixing and convergence under communication constraints in distributed settings. My current work investigates fully decentralized learning protocols that let nodes autonomously discover and collaborate with peers with similar data distributions, improving personalization in non-IID environments.</p>

  <div class="card">
    <div class="row"><h3>Personalization in Decentralized Learning <span class="status">Under review, ICLR 2026</span></h3><span class="meta">Aug 2025 – present</span></div>
    <ul>
      <li>Designed a decentralized P2P protocol that learns the latent statistical topology of non-IID networks via social triangulation, where nodes share ranked neighbor lists and train per-node embeddings with a ladder triplet loss.</li>
      <li>Matches validation-based baselines without a server, validation data, or cluster priors, using a four-slot explore–exploit sampler and a tent-weighted warmup anchor for stable affinity estimation.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <div class="row"><h3>Tiered Gossip Learning (TGL) <span class="status">Accepted, NeurIPS 2026</span></h3><span class="meta">Jan 2023 – May 2026</span></div>
    <ul>
      <li>Introduced a hybrid two-layer push-gossip-pull topology that achieves dense global model mixing and scalability by offloading aggregation to a dynamic relay backbone.</li>
      <li>Showed through theory and experiments that TGL matches the convergence rate of decentralized local SGD while cutting communication cost by up to 80% on non-IID vision and language tasks.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <div class="row"><h3>Byzantine-robust Federated Learning (FLAIR) · <a href="https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3579856.3582836">paper</a> · <a href="https://github.com/sharm438/FLAIR">code</a></h3><span class="meta">Mar 2020 – Aug 2022</span></div>
    <ul>
      <li>Developed an aggregation defense against model poisoning based on a novel statistic, the flip-score, to identify malicious clients.</li>
      <li>Evaluated against multiple models and datasets and against an adaptive whitebox attacker with full knowledge of the defense.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <div class="row"><h3>Lerna: Language-model Tuning of Genomic Error Correction · <a href="https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12859-021-04547-0">paper</a> · <a href="https://github.com/icanforce/lerna-genomics">code</a></h3><span class="meta">Aug 2019 – Jun 2020</span></div>
    <ul>
      <li>Built a framework that adapts non-directional Transformers to configure genomic error-correction tools, with an 18x inference speedup over state-of-the-art RNN models.</li>
      <li>Removed the need for a reference genome by using perplexity as a fitness measure, staying within 0.31% of the theoretical optimum alignment rate on Illumina and Nanopore datasets.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
